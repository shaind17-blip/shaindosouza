About Me

5 years leading teams.
8 years in the field.

Sales and leadership both come down to the same thing for me — architecture. Most teams underperform not because of talent, but because the foundation is not there. No clear process, no structured onboarding, no honest feedback loops, no realistic expectations.

Over 8 years across MENA, APAC, and global markets, I have learned that the role of a good leader is not to be the smartest person in the room. It is to build the room — the systems, the culture, and the clarity that let good people do great work.

I lead cross-functional teams spanning sales, business development, content, and creative production. I do not execute everything — I build the teams and frameworks so others can, and I stay close enough to the market to make sure strategy stays grounded in reality.

Get the architecture right and the results follow. That means building the systems, playbooks, and coaching culture — and then trusting the team you built to run with it.
