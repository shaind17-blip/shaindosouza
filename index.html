About Me

5 years leading teams.
8 years in the field.

Most leaders focus on the scoreboard. I focus on what puts the numbers there — the right people, the right processes, and a culture where accountability and ambition go hand in hand. That is how you build a region that performs consistently, not just occasionally.

Over 8 years across MENA, APAC, and global markets, I have built and led cross-functional teams spanning enterprise sales, business development, content, and creative production. My role is to set the direction, build the capability, and clear the path — so the team can run.

I stay close to the market, close to the client, and close to the people I lead. That proximity is what keeps strategy grounded and execution sharp.

I ended up in sales because connecting with people came naturally to me. Then I learned the hard way that there is a lot of unglamorous stuff that sits between the conversations — pipelines, processes, forecasting, follow-ups. Took me a while, but I got to loving all of it anyway.
